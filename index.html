ABOUT PUMA?

Puma is a global sportswear and lifestyle brand founded in 1948 in Germany by Rudolf Dassler. ... Known for its athletic shoes, apparel, and accessories, Puma has become one of the world’s leading sports brands, competing with companies like Nike and Adidas. The brand is recognized for its innovation, stylish designs, and collaborations with athletes, celebrities, and fashion icons. Puma’s products cater to a wide range of sports, including football, running, and motorsports, while also maintaining a strong presence in casual and streetwear fashion. Its iconic logo, the leaping puma, symbolizes speed, agility, and performance.
